Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Is BingoPay safe and secure? ▾

Yes, BingoPay is RBI compliant and uses bank-level security with end-to-end encryption to keep your data and transactions safe.

How do I register as a retailer? ▾

Sign up on our website or app, complete your KYC, and start transacting within minutes.

What documents are required for KYC? ▾

You'll need a valid ID proof, address proof, and a passport-size photo to complete KYC.

How do I add money to my wallet? ▾

You can add money via net banking, UPI, or by connecting your bank account directly in the app.

Do you provide customer support? ▾

Yes, our dedicated support team is available to help you with any queries, 9 AM - 9 PM, Mon - Sat.
